About R.C. Patel College of Engineering and Polytechnic, Shirpur

R.C. Patel College of Engineering and Polytechnic, Shirpur is a private, a non-autonomous engineering institute located in the Nashik region of Maharashtra, India. With a total sanctioned intake of 360 students across 4 engineering branches, it is a significant institution for engineering education. It operates under a minority quota (Linguistic Minority - Gujarathi). The institute code is 5433 and it participates in the MHT-CET CAP counselling process for admissions.
