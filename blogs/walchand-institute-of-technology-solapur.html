About Walchand Institute of Technology, Solapur

Walchand Institute of Technology, Solapur is a private, an autonomous engineering institute located in the Pune region of Maharashtra, India. With a total sanctioned intake of 660 students across 7 engineering branches, it is a significant institution for engineering education. It operates under a minority quota (Religious Minority - Jain). The institute code is 6265 and it participates in the MHT-CET CAP counselling process for admissions.
