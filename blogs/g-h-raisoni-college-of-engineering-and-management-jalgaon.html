About G H Raisoni College of Engineering and Management, Jalgaon

G H Raisoni College of Engineering and Management, Jalgaon is a private, an autonomous engineering institute located in the Nashik region of Maharashtra, India. With a total sanctioned intake of 780 students across 9 engineering branches, it is a significant institution for engineering education.
