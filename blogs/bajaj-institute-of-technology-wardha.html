About Bajaj Institute of Technology, Wardha

Bajaj Institute of Technology, Wardha is a private, a non-autonomous engineering institute located in the Nagpur region of Maharashtra, India. With a total sanctioned intake of 300 students across 4 engineering branches, it is a significant institution for engineering education. It operates under a minority quota (Linguistic Minority - Hindi). The institute code is 4649 and it participates in the MHT-CET CAP counselling process for admissions.
