About Jawaharlal Darda Institute of Engineering and Technology, Yavatmal

Jawaharlal Darda Institute of Engineering and Technology, Yavatmal is a private, an autonomous engineering institute located in the Amravati region of Maharashtra, India. With a total sanctioned intake of 390 students across 7 engineering branches, it is a significant institution for engineering education. It operates under a minority quota (Linguistic Minority - Hindi). The institute code is 1120 and it participates in the MHT-CET CAP counselling process for admissions.
