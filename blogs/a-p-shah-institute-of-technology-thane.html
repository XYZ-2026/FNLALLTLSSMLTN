About A. P. Shah Institute of Technology, Thane

A. P. Shah Institute of Technology, Thane is a private, a non-autonomous engineering institute located in the Mumbai region of Maharashtra, India. With a total sanctioned intake of 720 students across 6 engineering branches, it is a significant institution for engineering education. It operates under a minority quota (Religious Minority - Jain). The institute code is 3475 and it participates in the MHT-CET CAP counselling process for admissions.
