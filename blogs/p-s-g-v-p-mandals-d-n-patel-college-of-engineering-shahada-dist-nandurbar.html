About P.S.G.V.P. Mandal's D.N. Patel College of Engineering, Shahada, Dist. Nandurbar

P.S.G.V.P. Mandal's D.N. Patel College of Engineering, Shahada, Dist. Nandurbar is a private, a non-autonomous engineering institute located in the Nashik region of Maharashtra, India.
