About G H Raisoni College of Engineering & Management, Nagpur

G H Raisoni College of Engineering & Management, Nagpur is a private, an autonomous engineering institute located in the Nagpur region of Maharashtra, India. With a total sanctioned intake of 780 students across 9 engineering branches, it is a significant institution for engineering education. It operates under a minority quota (Linguistic Minority - Hindi). The institute code is 4142 and it participates in the MHT-CET CAP counselling process for admissions.
