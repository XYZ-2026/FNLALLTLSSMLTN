About Anjuman College of Engineering & Technology, Nagpur

Anjuman College of Engineering & Technology, Nagpur is a private, a non-autonomous engineering institute located in the Nagpur region of Maharashtra, India. With a total sanctioned intake of 420 students across 6 engineering branches, it is a significant institution for engineering education. It operates under a minority quota (Religious Minority - Muslim). The institute code is 4172 and it participates in the MHT-CET CAP counselling process for admissions.
