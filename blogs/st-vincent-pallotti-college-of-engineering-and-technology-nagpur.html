About ST. Vincent Pallotti College of Engineering & Technology, Nagpur

ST. Vincent Pallotti College of Engineering & Technology, Nagpur is a private, an autonomous engineering institute located in the Nagpur region of Maharashtra, India. With a total sanctioned intake of 780 students across 12 engineering branches, it is a significant institution for engineering education. It operates under a minority quota (Religious Minority - Christian). The institute code is 4174 and it participates in the MHT-CET CAP counselling process for admissions.
