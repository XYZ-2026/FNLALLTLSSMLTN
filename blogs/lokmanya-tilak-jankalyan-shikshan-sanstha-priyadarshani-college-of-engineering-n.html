About Lokmanya Tilak Jankalyan Shikshan Sanstha, Priyadarshani College of Engineering, Nagpur

Lokmanya Tilak Jankalyan Shikshan Sanstha, Priyadarshani College of Engineering, Nagpur is a private, an autonomous engineering institute located in the Nagpur region of Maharashtra, India. With a total sanctioned intake of 1440 students across 14 engineering branches, it is a significant institution for engineering education. It operates under a minority quota (Linguistic Minority - Hindi). The institute code is 4123 and it participates in the MHT-CET CAP counselling process for admissions.
