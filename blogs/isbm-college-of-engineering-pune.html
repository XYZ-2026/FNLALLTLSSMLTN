About ISBM College Of Engineering Pune

ISBM College Of Engineering Pune is a private, a non-autonomous engineering institute located in the Pune region of Maharashtra, India. With a total sanctioned intake of 630 students across 7 engineering branches, it is a significant institution for engineering education. It operates under a minority quota (Linguistic Minority - Hindi). The institute code is 6622 and it participates in the MHT-CET CAP counselling process for admissions.
