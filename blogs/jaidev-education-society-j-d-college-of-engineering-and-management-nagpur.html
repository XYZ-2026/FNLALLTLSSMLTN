About Jaidev Education Society, J D College of Engineering and Management, Nagpur

Jaidev Education Society, J D College of Engineering and Management, Nagpur is a private, an autonomous engineering institute located in the Nagpur region of Maharashtra, India. With a total sanctioned intake of 990 students across 9 engineering branches, it is a significant institution for engineering education. It operates under a minority quota (Linguistic Minority - Hindi). The institute code is 4138 and it participates in the MHT-CET CAP counselling process for admissions.
