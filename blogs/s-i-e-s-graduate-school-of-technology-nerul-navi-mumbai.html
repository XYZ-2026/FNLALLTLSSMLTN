About S.I.E.S. Graduate School of Technology, Nerul, Navi Mumbai

S.I.E.S. Graduate School of Technology, Nerul, Navi Mumbai is a private, an autonomous engineering institute located in the Mumbai region of Maharashtra, India. With a total sanctioned intake of 660 students across 7 engineering branches, it is a significant institution for engineering education. It operates under a minority quota (Linguistic Minority - Tamil). The institute code is 3211 and it participates in the MHT-CET CAP counselling process for admissions.
