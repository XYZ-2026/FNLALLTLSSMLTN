About Mahatma Education Society's Pillai College of Engineering, New Panvel

Mahatma Education Society's Pillai College of Engineering, New Panvel is a private, an autonomous engineering institute located in the Mumbai region of Maharashtra, India. With a total sanctioned intake of 660 students across 6 engineering branches, it is a significant institution for engineering education. It operates under a minority quota (Linguistic Minority - Malyalam). The institute code is 3207 and it participates in the MHT-CET CAP counselling process for admissions.
