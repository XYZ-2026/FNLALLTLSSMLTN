About Samridhi Sarwajanik Charitable Trust, Jhulelal Institute of Technology, Nagpur

Samridhi Sarwajanik Charitable Trust, Jhulelal Institute of Technology, Nagpur is a private, an autonomous engineering institute located in the Nagpur region of Maharashtra, India. With a total sanctioned intake of 720 students across 7 engineering branches, it is a significant institution for engineering education. It operates under a minority quota (Linguistic Minority - Sindhi). The institute code is 4139 and it participates in the MHT-CET CAP counselling process for admissions.
