What is the total intake capacity of Karmayogi Institute of Technology?

The average placement package at Karmayogi Institute of Technology is approximately 2–3 LPA. The college is classified as a Tier 4 institution based on placement performance.

How many branches does Karmayogi Institute of Technology offer?

Karmayogi Institute of Technology offers 4 engineering branches including Civil Engineering, Computer Science and Engineering, Electronics and Telecommunication Engg, Mechanical Engineering.

Is Karmayogi Institute of Technology an autonomous college?

No, Karmayogi Institute of Technology is not autonomous. The college status is: Un-Aided.

How many seats are available in Civil Engineering at Karmayogi Institute of Technology?
