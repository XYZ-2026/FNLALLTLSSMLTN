About Svkm's Shri Bhagubhai Mafatlal Polytechnic & College of Engineering

Svkm's Shri Bhagubhai Mafatlal Polytechnic & College of Engineering is a government, a non-autonomous engineering institute located in the Mumbai region of Maharashtra, India.
