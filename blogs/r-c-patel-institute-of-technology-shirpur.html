About R. C. Patel Institute of Technology, Shirpur

R. C. Patel Institute of Technology, Shirpur is a private, an autonomous engineering institute located in the Nashik region of Maharashtra, India. With a total sanctioned intake of 900 students across 9 engineering branches, it is a significant institution for engineering education. It operates under a minority quota (Linguistic Minority - Gujarathi). The institute code is 5172 and it participates in the MHT-CET CAP counselling process for admissions.
