MHT-CET Counselling 2027: Dates, CAP Rounds & Cutoff

MHT-CET counselling is the Centralised Admission Process (CAP) run by the State CET Cell for engineering seats in Maharashtra.

MHT-CET 2027 exam dates

As reported in November 2026 after the CET Cell announcement, PCM runs 16–26 April 2027 (first attempt) and 10–15 May 2027 (second attempt). CAP dates follow the results and are not out yet. Confirm on cetcell.mahacet.org.

How CAP works

  1. Register and upload documents, then complete verification.
  2. Check the provisional and final merit lists.
  3. Submit an option form for each round, best choice first.
  4. Check your allotment, then accept it or stay in for a later round as the brochure allows.
  5. Report to the college with original documents.

Seat types

S means state level, H home university and O other than home university. G is general and L is ladies, so GOPENS is a general open state-level seat. Our CAP 2026 data has four rounds.
