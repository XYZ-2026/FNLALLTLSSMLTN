JoSAA Counselling 2027: Process & Closing Ranks

JoSAA allots seats at IITs on JEE Advanced ranks, and at NITs, IIITs and GFTIs on JEE Main ranks.

JoSAA 2027 dates

The 2027 schedule is not out yet. Check josaa.nic.in and, for JEE Main dates, jeemain.nta.nic.in.

How JoSAA works

  1. Register and fill choices in order of preference.
  2. Check your seat after each round.
  3. Choose Freeze (keep the seat), Float (move up to any higher choice) or Slide (move up within the same institute).
  4. Pay the acceptance fee and finish document verification.

Quotas

NITs have Home State (HS) and Other State (OS) seats. IITs and IIITs use the All India (AI) quota. Our 2026 data covers five rounds for NITs, IIITs and GFTIs and six for IITs.
