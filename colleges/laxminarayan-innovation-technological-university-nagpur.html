Laxminarayan Innovation Technological University, Nagpur review: our verdict for 2027

Very high demand Laxminarayan Innovation Technological University, Nagpur is one of the most sought-after engineering colleges in Maharashtra. By its open-category CAP 2026 cutoffs it ranks #16 of 380 colleges in our data (top 4%). Its highest Round 1 cutoff was 99.45 percentile for Food Technology, and its lowest was 89.96 for Paper and Pulp Technology.

Strengths

  • University Autonomous institute: fees are usually much lower than at unaided private colleges.
  • Autonomous: the college sets its own syllabus and exams, so it can update courses faster than affiliated colleges.
  • Strong demand: in the top 4% of 380 Maharashtra colleges by cutoff.
  • 9 branches with open seats, so you have several ways in.
  • 84 All India seats: students outside Maharashtra can join on their JEE Main score.
  • Direct Second Year (DSE) seats for diploma holders.
  • Average package of 6–7 LPA in figures compiled by College Simplified.

Things to weigh

  • Branch matters a lot here: Round 1 cutoffs ranged 9.5 percentile points, from Food Technology to Paper and Pulp Technology.
  • Small intake of 270 seats: fewer seats means a single branch cutoff can move sharply from year to year.

Who should apply

Open-category students with an MHT-CET percentile of about 89.66 or higher have a realistic chance at some branch here, based on the last CAP 2026 round. For the top branch you need about 99.45 or higher. Reserved-category and ladies cutoffs are lower; see the category tables above.

Admission tips

  • Seats fill early: cutoffs barely moved after Round 1, so do not count on later rounds here.
