Pimpri Chinchwad College of Engineering and Research, Ravet review: our verdict for 2027

Very high demand Pimpri Chinchwad College of Engineering and Research, Ravet is one of the most sought-after engineering colleges in Maharashtra. By its open-category CAP 2026 cutoffs it ranks #19 of 380 colleges in our data (top 5%). Its highest Round 1 cutoff was 97.38 percentile for Information Technology, and its lowest was 93.97 for Civil Engineering.

Strengths

  • Autonomous: the college sets its own syllabus and exams, so it can update courses faster than affiliated colleges.
  • Strong demand: in the top 5% of 380 Maharashtra colleges by cutoff.
  • Information Technology closed at 97.38 percentile in Round 1 and 96.85 in Round 4.
  • 90 All India seats: students outside Maharashtra can join on their JEE Main score.
  • Direct Second Year (DSE) seats for diploma holders.
  • Average package of 5–6 LPA in figures compiled by College Simplified.

Things to weigh

  • Unaided (private) college: the yearly fee is set by the Fee Regulating Authority and is usually higher than at government colleges. Check the current fee before you lock a choice.

Who should apply

Open-category students with an MHT-CET percentile of about 94.23 or higher have a realistic chance at some branch here, based on the last CAP 2026 round. For the top branch you need about 97.38 or higher. Reserved-category and ladies cutoffs are lower; see the category tables above.

Admission tips

  • Seats fill early: cutoffs barely moved after Round 1, so do not count on later rounds here.
