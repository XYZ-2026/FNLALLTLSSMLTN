Government College of Engineering, Nagpur review: our verdict for 2027

High demand Government College of Engineering, Nagpur is a highly sought-after engineering college in Maharashtra. By its open-category CAP 2026 cutoffs it ranks #53 of 380 colleges in our data (top 14%). Its highest Round 1 cutoff was 97.00 percentile for Computer Science and Engineering, and its lowest was 88.56 for Civil Engineering.

Strengths

  • Government institute: fees are usually much lower than at unaided private colleges.
  • Strong demand: in the top 14% of 380 Maharashtra colleges by cutoff.
  • Computer Science and Engineering closed at 97.00 percentile in Round 1 and 97.64 in Round 4.
  • Direct Second Year (DSE) seats for diploma holders.
  • Average package of 5–6 LPA in figures compiled by College Simplified.

Things to weigh

  • Branch matters a lot here: Round 1 cutoffs ranged 8.4 percentile points, from Computer Science and Engineering to Civil Engineering.

Who should apply

Open-category students with an MHT-CET percentile of about 89.25 or higher have a realistic chance at some branch here, based on the last CAP 2026 round. For the top branch you need about 97.00 or higher. Reserved-category and ladies cutoffs are lower; see the category tables above.

Admission tips

  • Seats fill early: cutoffs barely moved after Round 1, so do not count on later rounds here.
  • Students from Rashtrasant Tukadoji Maharaj Nagpur University (home university) had the easier cutoff: home-university and other-university open seats differed by about 1.7 percentile points in Round 1. Check the GOPENH and GOPENO rows above.
