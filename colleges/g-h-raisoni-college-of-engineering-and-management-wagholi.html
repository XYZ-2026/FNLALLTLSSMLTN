G.H. Raisoni College of Engineering & Management, Wagholi review: our verdict for 2027

Above-average demand G.H. Raisoni College of Engineering & Management, Wagholi is an above-average engineering college in Maharashtra by demand. By its open-category CAP 2026 cutoffs it ranks #60 of 380 colleges in our data (top 16%). Its highest Round 1 cutoff was 92.82 percentile for Computer Engineering, and its lowest was 84.47 for Civil Engineering.

Strengths

  • Autonomous: the college sets its own syllabus and exams, so it can update courses faster than affiliated colleges.
  • Computer Engineering closed at 92.82 percentile in Round 1 and 92.65 in Round 4.
  • 10 branches with open seats, so you have several ways in.
  • 57 All India seats: students outside Maharashtra can join on their JEE Main score.
  • Direct Second Year (DSE) seats for diploma holders.
  • Average package of 4–5 LPA in figures compiled by College Simplified.

Things to weigh

  • Unaided (private) college: the yearly fee is set by the Fee Regulating Authority and is usually higher than at government colleges. Check the current fee before you lock a choice.
  • Hindi minority institute: part of the intake is kept for that community, so fewer seats are open to everyone else.
  • Branch matters a lot here: Round 1 cutoffs ranged 8.4 percentile points, from Computer Engineering to Civil Engineering.

Who should apply

Open-category students with an MHT-CET percentile of about 85.79 or higher have a realistic chance at some branch here, based on the last CAP 2026 round. For the top branch you need about 92.82 or higher. Reserved-category and ladies cutoffs are lower; see the category tables above.

Admission tips

  • Seats fill early: cutoffs barely moved after Round 1, so do not count on later rounds here.
