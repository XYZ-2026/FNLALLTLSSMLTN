Institute of Chemical Technology, Matunga review: our verdict for 2027

Very high demand Institute of Chemical Technology, Matunga is one of the most sought-after engineering colleges in Maharashtra. By its open-category CAP 2026 cutoffs it ranks #5 of 380 colleges in our data (top 1%). Its highest Round 1 cutoff was 99.90 percentile for Pharmaceuticals Chemistry and Technology, and its lowest was 98.26 for Fibres and Textile Processing Technology.

Strengths

  • Autonomous: the college sets its own syllabus and exams, so it can update courses faster than affiliated colleges.
  • Strong demand: in the top 1% of 380 Maharashtra colleges by cutoff.
  • 8 branches with open seats, so you have several ways in.
  • 63 All India seats: students outside Maharashtra can join on their JEE Main score.
  • Average package of 8–9 LPA in figures compiled by College Simplified.

Things to weigh

  • Small intake of 209 seats: fewer seats means a single branch cutoff can move sharply from year to year.

Who should apply

Open-category students with an MHT-CET percentile of about 98.01 or higher have a realistic chance at some branch here, based on the last CAP 2026 round. For the top branch you need about 99.90 or higher. Reserved-category and ladies cutoffs are lower; see the category tables above.

Admission tips

  • Seats fill early: cutoffs barely moved after Round 1, so do not count on later rounds here.
