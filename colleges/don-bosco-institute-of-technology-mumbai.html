Don Bosco Institute of Technology, Mumbai review: our verdict for 2027

High demand Don Bosco Institute of Technology, Mumbai is a highly sought-after engineering college in Maharashtra. Its highest Round 1 cutoff was 95.49 percentile for Computer Engineering, and its lowest was 93.44 for Mechanical Engineering.

Strengths

  • Autonomous: the college sets its own syllabus and exams, so it can update courses faster than affiliated colleges.
  • Strong demand: in the top 11% of 380 Maharashtra colleges by cutoff.
  • Computer Engineering closed at 95.49 percentile in Round 1 and 95.13 in Round 4.
  • 25 All India seats: students outside Maharashtra can join on their JEE Main score.
  • Direct Second Year (DSE) seats for diploma holders.
  • Average package of 5–6 LPA in figures compiled by College Simplified.

Things to weigh

  • Unaided (private) college: the yearly fee is set by the Fee Regulating Authority and is usually higher than at government colleges. Check the current fee before you lock a choice.
  • Roman Catholics minority institute: part of the intake is kept for that community, so fewer seats are open to everyone else.

Who should apply

Open-category students with an MHT-CET percentile of about 91.15 or higher have a realistic chance at some branch here, based on the last CAP 2026 round. For the top branch you need about 95.49 or higher. Reserved-category and ladies cutoffs are lower; see the category tables above.
