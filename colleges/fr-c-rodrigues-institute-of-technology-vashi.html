FR. C. Rodrigues Institute of Technology, Vashi review: our verdict for 2027

High demand FR. C. Rodrigues Institute of Technology, Vashi is a highly sought-after engineering college in Maharashtra. By its open-category CAP 2026 cutoffs it ranks #22 of 380 colleges in our data (top 6%). Its highest Round 1 cutoff was 97.17 percentile for Computer Engineering, and its lowest was 95.13 for Electrical Engineering.

Strengths

  • Autonomous: the college sets its own syllabus and exams, so it can update courses faster than affiliated colleges.
  • Strong demand: in the top 6% of 380 Maharashtra colleges by cutoff.
  • Computer Engineering closed at 97.17 percentile in Round 1 and 96.96 in Round 4.
  • 21 All India seats: students outside Maharashtra can join on their JEE Main score.
  • Direct Second Year (DSE) seats for diploma holders.
  • Average package of 5–6 LPA in figures compiled by College Simplified.

Things to weigh

  • Unaided (private) college: the yearly fee is set by the Fee Regulating Authority and is usually higher than at government colleges. Check the current fee before you lock a choice.
  • Christian minority institute: part of the intake is kept for that community, so fewer seats are open to everyone else.

Who should apply

Open-category students with an MHT-CET percentile of about 95.14 or higher have a realistic chance at some branch here, based on the last CAP 2026 round. For the top branch you need about 97.17 or higher. Reserved-category and ladies cutoffs are lower; see the category tables above.

Admission tips

  • Seats fill early: cutoffs barely moved after Round 1, so do not count on later rounds here.
