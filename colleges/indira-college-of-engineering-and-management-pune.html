Strengths

  • Autonomous: the college sets its own syllabus and exams, so it can update courses faster than affiliated colleges.
  • Strong demand: in the top 15% of 380 Maharashtra colleges by cutoff.
  • Computer Engineering closed at 92.59 percentile in Round 1 and 92.12 in Round 4.
  • 63 All India seats: students outside Maharashtra can join on their JEE Main score.

  • Average package of 3–4 LPA in figures compiled by College Simplified.
