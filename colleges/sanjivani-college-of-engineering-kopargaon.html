Sanjivani College of Engineering, Kopargaon review: our verdict for 2027

Above-average demand Sanjivani College of Engineering, Kopargaon is an above-average engineering college in Maharashtra by demand. By its open-category CAP 2026 cutoffs it ranks #58 of 380 colleges in our data (top 15%). Its highest Round 1 cutoff was 94.51 percentile for Computer Engineering, and its lowest was 77.23 for Structural Engineering.

Strengths

  • Autonomous: the college sets its own syllabus and exams, so it can update courses faster than affiliated colleges.
  • Computer Engineering closed at 94.51 percentile in Round 1 and 94.66 in Round 4.
  • 8 branches with open seats, so you have several ways in.
  • 135 All India seats: students outside Maharashtra can join on their JEE Main score.
  • Direct Second Year (DSE) seats for diploma holders.
  • Average package of 4–5 LPA in figures compiled by College Simplified.

Things to weigh

  • Unaided (private) college: the yearly fee is set by the Fee Regulating Authority and is usually higher than at government colleges. Check the current fee before you lock a choice.
  • Branch matters a lot here: Round 1 cutoffs ranged 17.3 percentile points, from Computer Engineering to Structural Engineering.

Who should apply

Open-category students with an MHT-CET percentile of about 82.49 or higher have a realistic chance at some branch here, based on the last CAP 2026 round. For the top branch you need about 94.51 or higher. Reserved-category and ladies cutoffs are lower; see the category tables above.

Admission tips

  • Seats fill early: cutoffs barely moved after Round 1, so do not count on later rounds here.
