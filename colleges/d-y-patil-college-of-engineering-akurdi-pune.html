D.Y. Patil College of Engineering Akurdi, Pune review: our verdict for 2027

Very high demand D.Y. Patil College of Engineering Akurdi, Pune is one of the most sought-after engineering colleges in Maharashtra. By its open-category CAP 2026 cutoffs it ranks #15 of 380 colleges in our data (top 4%). Its highest Round 1 cutoff was 98.13 percentile for Computer Engineering, and its lowest was 94.42 for Instrumentation Engineering.

Strengths

  • Autonomous: the college sets its own syllabus and exams, so it can update courses faster than affiliated colleges.
  • Strong demand: in the top 4% of 380 Maharashtra colleges by cutoff.
  • Computer Engineering closed at 98.13 percentile in Round 1 and 98.00 in Round 4.
  • 8 branches with open seats, so you have several ways in.
  • 162 All India seats: students outside Maharashtra can join on their JEE Main score.
  • Direct Second Year (DSE) seats for diploma holders.
  • Average package of 6–7 LPA in figures compiled by College Simplified.
  • Large intake of 1,080 seats across all branches.

Things to weigh

  • Unaided (private) college: the yearly fee is set by the Fee Regulating Authority and is usually higher than at government colleges. Check the current fee before you lock a choice.

Who should apply

Open-category students with an MHT-CET percentile of about 95.90 or higher have a realistic chance at some branch here, based on the last CAP 2026 round. For the top branch you need about 98.13 or higher. Reserved-category and ladies cutoffs are lower; see the category tables above.

Admission tips

  • Seats fill early: cutoffs barely moved after Round 1, so do not count on later rounds here.
