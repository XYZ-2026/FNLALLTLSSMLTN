Its highest Round 1 cutoff was 99.59 percentile for Computer Science and Engineering, and its lowest was 98.26 for Civil Engineering.

Strengths

  • Government-Aided Autonomous institute: fees are usually much lower than at unaided private colleges.
  • Autonomous: the college sets its own syllabus and exams, so it can update courses faster than affiliated colleges.
  • Strong demand: in the top 2% of 380 Maharashtra colleges by cutoff.
  • Computer Science and Engineering closed at 99.59 percentile in Round 1 and 99.54 in Round 4.
  • 11 branches with open seats, so you have several ways in.
  • Direct Second Year (DSE) seats for diploma holders.
  • Average package of 10–11 LPA in figures compiled by College Simplified.

Who should apply

Open-category students with an MHT-CET percentile of about 98.24 or higher have a realistic chance at some branch here, based on the last CAP 2026 round. For the top branch you need about 99.59 or higher. Reserved-category and ladies cutoffs are lower; see the category tables above.

Admission tips

  • Seats fill early: cutoffs barely moved after Round 1, so do not count on later rounds here.
  • Girls: ladies open seats closed about 0.6 percentile points lower than general open seats in Round 1.
