Government College of Engineering, Amravati review: our verdict for 2027

High demand Government College of Engineering, Amravati is a highly sought-after engineering college in Maharashtra.   • Government Autonomous institute: fees are usually much lower than at unaided private colleges.

  • Strong demand: in the top 8% of 380 Maharashtra colleges by cutoff.
