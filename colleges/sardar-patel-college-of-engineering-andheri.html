Sardar Patel College of Engineering, Andheri review: our verdict for 2027

Very high demand Sardar Patel College of Engineering, Andheri is one of the most sought-after engineering colleges in Maharashtra. By its open-category CAP 2026 cutoffs it ranks #12 of 380 colleges in our data (top 3%). Its highest Round 1 cutoff was 99.23 percentile for Electrical Engineering, and its lowest was 98.44 for Civil Engineering.

Strengths

  • Government-Aided Autonomous institute: fees are usually much lower than at unaided private colleges.
  • Autonomous: the college sets its own syllabus and exams, so it can update courses faster than affiliated colleges.
  • Strong demand: in the top 3% of 380 Maharashtra colleges by cutoff.
  • Direct Second Year (DSE) seats for diploma holders.
  • Average package of 6–7 LPA in figures compiled by College Simplified.

Things to weigh

  • Small intake of 180 seats: fewer seats means a single branch cutoff can move sharply from year to year.

Who should apply

Open-category students with an MHT-CET percentile of about 98.79 or higher have a realistic chance at some branch here, based on the last CAP 2026 round. For the top branch you need about 99.23 or higher. Reserved-category and ladies cutoffs are lower; see the category tables above.

Admission tips

  • Seats fill early: cutoffs barely moved after Round 1, so do not count on later rounds here.
