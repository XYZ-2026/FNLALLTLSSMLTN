Strengths

  • Autonomous: the college sets its own syllabus and exams, so it can update courses faster than affiliated colleges.
  • Strong demand: in the top 14% of 380 Maharashtra colleges by cutoff.
  • Computer Engineering closed at 94.45 percentile in Round 1 and 94.09 in Round 4.
