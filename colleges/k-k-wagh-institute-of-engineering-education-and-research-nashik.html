K. K. Wagh Institute of Engineering Education and Research, Nashik review: our verdict for 2027

High demand K. K. Wagh Institute of Engineering Education and Research, Nashik is a highly sought-after engineering college in Maharashtra. By its open-category CAP 2026 cutoffs it ranks #40 of 380 colleges in our data (top 11%). Its highest Round 1 cutoff was 95.94 percentile for Computer Engineering, and its lowest was 87.73 for Civil Engineering.

Strengths

  • Autonomous: the college sets its own syllabus and exams, so it can update courses faster than affiliated colleges.
  • Strong demand: in the top 11% of 380 Maharashtra colleges by cutoff.
  • Computer Engineering closed at 95.94 percentile in Round 1 and 96.51 in Round 4.
  • 10 branches with open seats, so you have several ways in.
  • 171 All India seats: students outside Maharashtra can join on their JEE Main score.
  • Direct Second Year (DSE) seats for diploma holders.
  • Average package of 5–6 LPA in figures compiled by College Simplified.
  • Large intake of 1,140 seats across all branches.

Things to weigh

  • Unaided (private) college: the yearly fee is set by the Fee Regulating Authority and is usually higher than at government colleges. Check the current fee before you lock a choice.
  • Branch matters a lot here: Round 1 cutoffs ranged 8.2 percentile points, from Computer Engineering to Civil Engineering.

Who should apply

Open-category students with an MHT-CET percentile of about 88.58 or higher have a realistic chance at some branch here, based on the last CAP 2026 round. For the top branch you need about 95.94 or higher. Reserved-category and ladies cutoffs are lower; see the category tables above.

Admission tips

  • Seats fill early: cutoffs barely moved after Round 1, so do not count on later rounds here.
