Vishwakarma Institute of Technology, Pune review: our verdict for 2027

Very high demand Vishwakarma Institute of Technology, Pune is one of the most sought-after engineering colleges in Maharashtra. By its open-category CAP 2026 cutoffs it ranks #10 of 380 colleges in our data (top 3%). Its highest Round 1 cutoff was 99.09 percentile for Computer Engineering, and its lowest was 96.77 for Civil Engineering.

Strengths

  • Autonomous: the college sets its own syllabus and exams, so it can update courses faster than affiliated colleges.
  • Strong demand: in the top 3% of 380 Maharashtra colleges by cutoff.
  • Computer Engineering closed at 99.09 percentile in Round 1 and 99.04 in Round 4.
  • 12 branches with open seats, so you have several ways in.
  • 423 All India seats: students outside Maharashtra can join on their JEE Main score.
  • Direct Second Year (DSE) seats for diploma holders.
  • Average package of 7–8 LPA in figures compiled by College Simplified.
  • Large intake of 2,820 seats across all branches.

Things to weigh

  • Unaided (private) college: the yearly fee is set by the Fee Regulating Authority and is usually higher than at government colleges. Check the current fee before you lock a choice.

Who should apply

Open-category students with an MHT-CET percentile of about 97.43 or higher have a realistic chance at some branch here, based on the last CAP 2026 round. For the top branch you need about 99.09 or higher. Reserved-category and ladies cutoffs are lower; see the category tables above.

Admission tips

  • Seats fill early: cutoffs barely moved after Round 1, so do not count on later rounds here.
