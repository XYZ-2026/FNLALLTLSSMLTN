Shri Bhagubhai Mafatlal Polytechnic & College of Engineering review: our verdict for 2027

Its highest Round 1 cutoff was 93.40 percentile for Computer Engineering, and its lowest was 91.42 for Information Technology.

Strengths

  • Strong demand: in the top 15% of 380 Maharashtra colleges by cutoff.

  • Average package of 4–5 LPA in figures compiled by College Simplified.

Things to weigh

  • Unaided (private) college: the yearly fee is set by the Fee Regulating Authority and is usually higher than at government colleges. Check the current fee before you lock a choice.
  • Gujarathi minority institute: part of the intake is kept for that community, so fewer seats are open to everyone else.

Who should apply

Open-category students with an MHT-CET percentile of about 91.54 or higher have a realistic chance at some branch here, based on the last CAP 2026 round. For the top branch you need about 93.40 or higher. Reserved-category and ladies cutoffs are lower; see the category tables above.

Admission tips

  • Seats fill early: cutoffs barely moved after Round 1, so do not count on later rounds here.
  • Students from outside Mumbai University had the easier cutoff: home-university and other-university open seats differed by about 4.6 percentile points in Round 1. Check the GOPENH and GOPENO rows above.
