Xavier Institute of Engineering, Mahim review: our verdict for 2027

High demand Xavier Institute of Engineering, Mahim is a highly sought-after engineering college in Maharashtra. By its open-category CAP 2026 cutoffs it ranks #43 of 380 colleges in our data (top 11%). Its highest Round 1 cutoff was 95.09 percentile for Computer Engineering, and its lowest was 93.81 for Computer Science and Engineering (Internet of Things and Cyber Security Including Block Chain Technology).

Strengths

  • Autonomous: the college sets its own syllabus and exams, so it can update courses faster than affiliated colleges.
  • Strong demand: in the top 11% of 380 Maharashtra colleges by cutoff.
  • Computer Engineering closed at 95.09 percentile in Round 1 and 94.18 in Round 4.
  • 11 All India seats: students outside Maharashtra can join on their JEE Main score.
  • Direct Second Year (DSE) seats for diploma holders.
  • Average package of 5–6 LPA in figures compiled by College Simplified.

Things to weigh

  • Unaided (private) college: the yearly fee is set by the Fee Regulating Authority and is usually higher than at government colleges. Check the current fee before you lock a choice.
  • Christian minority institute: part of the intake is kept for that community, so fewer seats are open to everyone else.
  • Small intake of 240 seats: fewer seats means a single branch cutoff can move sharply from year to year.

Who should apply

Open-category students with an MHT-CET percentile of about 90.91 or higher have a realistic chance at some branch here, based on the last CAP 2026 round. For the top branch you need about 95.09 or higher. Reserved-category and ladies cutoffs are lower; see the category tables above.

Admission tips

  • Cutoffs fell in later rounds: the typical branch dropped 1.8 percentile points by the last round. If you are a little below the Round 1 cutoff, keep this college in your option form.
