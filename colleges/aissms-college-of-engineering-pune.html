AISSMS College of Engineering, Pune review: our verdict for 2027

High demand AISSMS College of Engineering, Pune is a highly sought-after engineering college in Maharashtra. By its open-category CAP 2026 cutoffs it ranks #23 of 380 colleges in our data (top 6%). Its highest Round 1 cutoff was 97.67 percentile for Computer Engineering, and its lowest was 91.84 for Chemical Engineering.

Strengths

  • Autonomous: the college sets its own syllabus and exams, so it can update courses faster than affiliated colleges.
  • Strong demand: in the top 6% of 380 Maharashtra colleges by cutoff.
  • Computer Engineering closed at 97.67 percentile in Round 1 and 97.74 in Round 4.
  • 10 branches with open seats, so you have several ways in.
  • 117 All India seats: students outside Maharashtra can join on their JEE Main score.
  • Direct Second Year (DSE) seats for diploma holders.
  • Average package of 5–6 LPA in figures compiled by College Simplified.

Things to weigh

  • Unaided (private) college: the yearly fee is set by the Fee Regulating Authority and is usually higher than at government colleges. Check the current fee before you lock a choice.

Who should apply

Open-category students with an MHT-CET percentile of about 92.74 or higher have a realistic chance at some branch here, based on the last CAP 2026 round. For the top branch you need about 97.67 or higher. Reserved-category and ladies cutoffs are lower; see the category tables above.

Admission tips

  • Seats fill early: cutoffs barely moved after Round 1, so do not count on later rounds here.
