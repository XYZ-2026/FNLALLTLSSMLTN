A. P. Shah Institute of Technology, Thane review: our verdict for 2027

Above-average demand A. P. Shah Institute of Technology, Thane is an above-average engineering college in Maharashtra by demand. By its open-category CAP 2026 cutoffs it ranks #65 of 380 colleges in our data (top 17%). Its highest Round 1 cutoff was 92.51 percentile for Computer Engineering, and its lowest was 84.24 for Civil Engineering.

Strengths

  • Computer Engineering closed at 92.51 percentile in Round 1 and 95.08 in Round 4.
  • 6 branches with open seats, so you have several ways in.
  • 27 All India seats: students outside Maharashtra can join on their JEE Main score.
  • Direct Second Year (DSE) seats for diploma holders.
  • Average package of 4–5 LPA in figures compiled by College Simplified.

Things to weigh

  • Unaided (private) college: the yearly fee is set by the Fee Regulating Authority and is usually higher than at government colleges. Check the current fee before you lock a choice.
  • Jain minority institute: part of the intake is kept for that community, so fewer seats are open to everyone else.
  • Branch matters a lot here: Round 1 cutoffs ranged 8.3 percentile points, from Computer Engineering to Civil Engineering.

Who should apply

Open-category students with an MHT-CET percentile of about 84.78 or higher have a realistic chance at some branch here, based on the last CAP 2026 round. For the top branch you need about 92.51 or higher. Reserved-category and ladies cutoffs are lower; see the category tables above.

Admission tips

  • Seats fill early: cutoffs barely moved after Round 1, so do not count on later rounds here.
  • Students from outside Mumbai University had the easier cutoff: home-university and other-university open seats differed by about 5.1 percentile points in Round 1. Check the GOPENH and GOPENO rows above.
