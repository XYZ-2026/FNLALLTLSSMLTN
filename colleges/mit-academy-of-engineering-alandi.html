MIT Academy of Engineering, Alandi review: our verdict for 2027

High demand MIT Academy of Engineering, Alandi is a highly sought-after engineering college in Maharashtra. By its open-category CAP 2026 cutoffs it ranks #29 of 380 colleges in our data (top 8%). Its highest Round 1 cutoff was 96.56 percentile for Computer Engineering, and its lowest was 92.16 for Civil Engineering.

Strengths

  • Autonomous: the college sets its own syllabus and exams, so it can update courses faster than affiliated colleges.
  • Strong demand: in the top 8% of 380 Maharashtra colleges by cutoff.
  • Computer Engineering closed at 96.56 percentile in Round 1 and 96.32 in Round 4.
  • 13 branches with open seats, so you have several ways in.
  • 198 All India seats: students outside Maharashtra can join on their JEE Main score.
  • Direct Second Year (DSE) seats for diploma holders.
  • Average package of 5–6 LPA in figures compiled by College Simplified.
  • Large intake of 1,320 seats across all branches.

Things to weigh

  • Unaided (private) college: the yearly fee is set by the Fee Regulating Authority and is usually higher than at government colleges. Check the current fee before you lock a choice.

Who should apply

Open-category students with an MHT-CET percentile of about 91.83 or higher have a realistic chance at some branch here, based on the last CAP 2026 round. For the top branch you need about 96.56 or higher. Reserved-category and ladies cutoffs are lower; see the category tables above.

Admission tips
