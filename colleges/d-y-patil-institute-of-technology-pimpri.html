D.Y. Patil Institute of Technology, Pimpri review: our verdict for 2027

High demand D.Y. Patil Institute of Technology, Pimpri is a highly sought-after engineering college in Maharashtra. By its open-category CAP 2026 cutoffs it ranks #20 of 380 colleges in our data (top 5%). Its highest Round 1 cutoff was 97.41 percentile for Computer Engineering, and its lowest was 90.97 for Civil Engineering.

Strengths

  • Strong demand: in the top 5% of 380 Maharashtra colleges by cutoff.
  • Computer Engineering closed at 97.41 percentile in Round 1 and 97.53 in Round 4.
  • 9 branches with open seats, so you have several ways in.
  • 167 All India seats: students outside Maharashtra can join on their JEE Main score.
  • Direct Second Year (DSE) seats for diploma holders.
  • Average package of 5–6 LPA in figures compiled by College Simplified.
  • Large intake of 1,110 seats across all branches.

Things to weigh

  • Unaided (private) college: the yearly fee is set by the Fee Regulating Authority and is usually higher than at government colleges. Check the current fee before you lock a choice.

Who should apply

Open-category students with an MHT-CET percentile of about 91.42 or higher have a realistic chance at some branch here, based on the last CAP 2026 round. For the top branch you need about 97.41 or higher. Reserved-category and ladies cutoffs are lower; see the category tables above.

Admission tips

  • Seats fill early: cutoffs barely moved after Round 1, so do not count on later rounds here.
  • Students from Savitribai Phule Pune University (home university) had the easier cutoff: home-university and other-university open seats differed by about 0.8 percentile points in Round 1. Check the GOPENH and GOPENO rows above.
