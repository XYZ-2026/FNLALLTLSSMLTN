Cummins College of Engineering for Women, Pune review: our verdict for 2027

Very high demand Cummins College of Engineering for Women, Pune is one of the most sought-after engineering colleges in Maharashtra. Its highest Round 1 cutoff was 99.40 percentile for Computer Engineering, and its lowest was 98.12 for Instrumentation and Control Engineering.

Strengths

  • Autonomous: the college sets its own syllabus and exams, so it can update courses faster than affiliated colleges.
  • Strong demand: in the top 2% of 380 Maharashtra colleges by cutoff.
  • Computer Engineering closed at 99.40 percentile in Round 1 and 99.35 in Round 4.
  • Women-only college: every seat is a ladies seat, so you compete only with other women candidates.
  • Direct Second Year (DSE) seats for diploma holders.
  • Average package of 12–13 LPA in figures compiled by College Simplified.

Things to weigh

  • Unaided (private) college: the yearly fee is set by the Fee Regulating Authority and is usually higher than at government colleges. Check the current fee before you lock a choice.

Who should apply

Open-category women with an MHT-CET percentile of about 97.88 or higher have a realistic chance at some branch here, based on the last CAP 2026 round. For the top branch you need about 99.40 or higher. Reserved-category cutoffs are lower; see the category tables above.

Admission tips

  • Seats fill early: cutoffs barely moved after Round 1, so do not count on later rounds here.
