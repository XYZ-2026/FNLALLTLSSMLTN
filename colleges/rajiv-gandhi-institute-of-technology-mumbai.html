High demand Rajiv Gandhi Institute of Technology, Mumbai is a highly sought-after engineering college in Maharashtra. By its open-category CAP 2026 cutoffs it ranks #36 of 380 colleges in our data (top 9%). Its highest Round 1 cutoff was 95.81 percentile for Computer Engineering, and its lowest was 93.43 for Electronics and Telecommunication Engg.

Strengths

  • Strong demand: in the top 9% of 380 Maharashtra colleges by cutoff.
  • Computer Engineering closed at 95.81 percentile in Round 1 and 95.36 in Round 4.
  • 72 All India seats: students outside Maharashtra can join on their JEE Main score.
  • Direct Second Year (DSE) seats for diploma holders.
  • Average package of 5–6 LPA in figures compiled by College Simplified.
