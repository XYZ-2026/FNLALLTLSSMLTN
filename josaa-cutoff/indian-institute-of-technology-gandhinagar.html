Indian Institute of Technology Gandhinagar review: our verdict for 2027

Moderate demand Indian Institute of Technology Gandhinagar is a mid-range IIT in JoSAA. By its open-category closing ranks across branches it ranks #12 of 24 IITs in our 2026 data (top 50%). The most competitive seat was Computer Science and Engineering at JEE Advanced rank 2,315; the least competitive was Materials Engineering at 12,873.

Strengths

  • Centrally funded IIT: fees are subsidised and the degree is widely recognised by employers and for higher studies.
  • 8 programmes with open seats, so a wide range of ranks can get in.
  • Placements in figures compiled by College Simplified: avg package ₹17 LPA, median package ₹13 LPA, highest package ₹70 LPA, 84% placed.

Open-category, gender-neutral candidates with a JEE Advanced rank up to about 12,873 had a seat here in 2026; the top branch needed about 2,315 or better. Category ranks, female-only seats and PwD ranks are in the tables above.
