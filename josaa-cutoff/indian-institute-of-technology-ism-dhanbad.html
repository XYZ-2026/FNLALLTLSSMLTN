Indian Institute of Technology (ISM) Dhanbad review: our verdict for 2027

Easier admission Indian Institute of Technology (ISM) Dhanbad is one of the easier IITs to get into in JoSAA. By its open-category closing ranks across branches it ranks #17 of 24 IITs in our 2026 data (bottom 30%). The most competitive seat was Computer Science and Engineering at JEE Advanced rank 4,167; the least competitive was Chemical Science (5 Years, Integrated Bachelor of Science-Master of Science) at 18,977.

Strengths

  • Centrally funded IIT: fees are subsidised and the degree is widely recognised by employers and for higher studies.
  • 18 programmes with open seats, so a wide range of ranks can get in.
  • Placements in figures compiled by College Simplified: avg package ₹18 LPA, median package ₹14 LPA, highest package ₹1 Cr, 86% placed.

Who should apply

Open-category, gender-neutral candidates with a JEE Advanced rank up to about 18,977 had a seat here in 2026; the top branch needed about 4,167 or better. Category ranks, female-only seats and PwD ranks are in the tables above.

Admission tips

  • Computer Science closed at JEE Advanced rank 4,167 (All India quota, Round 6).
  • Branch decides the rank you need: closing ranks ran from 4,167 to 18,977.
  • Girls: female-only seats closed at ranks about 1.7× the gender-neutral ranks.
