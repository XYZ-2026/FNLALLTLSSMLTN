Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur review: our verdict for 2027

Above-average demand Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur is an above-average IIT by demand in JoSAA. By its open-category closing ranks across branches it ranks #7 of 24 IITs in our 2026 data (top 29%). The most competitive seat was Computer Science and Engineering at JEE Advanced rank 509; the least competitive was Applied Geology (4 Years, Bachelor of Science) at 15,872.

Strengths

  • Centrally funded IIT: fees are subsidised and the degree is widely recognised by employers and for higher studies.
  • Computer Science closed at JEE Advanced rank 509 (All India quota, Round 6).
  • 22 programmes with open seats, so a wide range of ranks can get in.
  • Placements in figures compiled by College Simplified: avg package ₹22 LPA, median package ₹18 LPA, highest package ₹2.4 Cr, 90% placed.

Things to weigh

  • Branch decides the rank you need: closing ranks ran from 509 to 15,872.

Who should apply

Open-category, gender-neutral candidates with a JEE Advanced rank up to about 15,872 had a seat here in 2026; the top branch needed about 509 or better. Category ranks, female-only seats and PwD ranks are in the tables above.

Admission tips

  • Girls: female-only seats closed at ranks about 2.0× the gender-neutral ranks.
