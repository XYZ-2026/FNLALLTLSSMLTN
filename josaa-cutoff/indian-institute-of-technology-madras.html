Indian Institute of Technology Madras review: our verdict for 2027

High demand Indian Institute of Technology Madras is a highly sought-after IIT in JoSAA. By its open-category closing ranks across branches it ranks #2 of 24 IITs in our 2026 data (top 8%). The most competitive seat was Computer Science and Engineering at JEE Advanced rank 151; the least competitive was Biological Science (4 Years, Bachelor of Science) at 12,204.

Strengths

  • Centrally funded IIT: fees are subsidised and the degree is widely recognised by employers and for higher studies.
  • Strong demand: in the top 8% of 24 IITs by closing rank.
  • Computer Science closed at JEE Advanced rank 151 (All India quota, Round 6).
  • 17 programmes with open seats, so a wide range of ranks can get in.
  • Placements in figures compiled by College Simplified: avg package ₹25 LPA, median package ₹21 LPA, highest package ₹1.3 Cr, 95% placed.

Things to weigh

  • Branch decides the rank you need: closing ranks ran from 151 to 12,204.

Who should apply

Open-category, gender-neutral candidates with a JEE Advanced rank up to about 12,204 had a seat here in 2026; the top branch needed about 151 or better. Category ranks, female-only seats and PwD ranks are in the tables above.

Admission tips

  • Girls: female-only seats closed at ranks about 2.3× the gender-neutral ranks.
