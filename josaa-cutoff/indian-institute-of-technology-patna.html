Moderate demand Indian Institute of Technology Patna is a mid-range IIT in JoSAA. By its open-category closing ranks across branches it ranks #13 of 24 IITs in our 2026 data (bottom 47%). The most competitive seat was Computer Science and Engineering at JEE Advanced rank 3,635; the least competitive was B. Tech in CE. - M. Tech. in Geotechnical Engineering (5 Years, B.Tech. + M.Tech./MS (Dual Degree)) at 17,820.

Strengths

  • Centrally funded IIT: fees are subsidised and the degree is widely recognised by employers and for higher studies.

Open-category, gender-neutral candidates with a JEE Advanced rank up to about 17,820 had a seat here in 2026; the top branch needed about 3,635 or better. Category ranks, female-only seats and PwD ranks are in the tables above.
