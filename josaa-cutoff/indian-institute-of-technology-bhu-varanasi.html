Indian Institute of Technology (BHU) Varanasi review: our verdict for 2027

Moderate demand Indian Institute of Technology (BHU) Varanasi is a mid-range IIT in JoSAA. By its open-category closing ranks across branches it ranks #9 of 24 IITs in our 2026 data (top 38%). The most competitive seat was Computer Science and Engineering at JEE Advanced rank 1,439; the least competitive was Pharmaceutical Engineering & Technology at 15,894.

Strengths

  • Centrally funded IIT: fees are subsidised and the degree is widely recognised by employers and for higher studies.
  • 16 programmes with open seats, so a wide range of ranks can get in.
  • Placements in figures compiled by College Simplified: avg package ₹19 LPA, median package ₹15 LPA, highest package ₹1.7 Cr, 88% placed.

Things to weigh

  • Branch decides the rank you need: closing ranks ran from 1,439 to 15,894.

Who should apply

Open-category, gender-neutral candidates with a JEE Advanced rank up to about 15,894 had a seat here in 2026; the top branch needed about 1,439 or better. Category ranks, female-only seats and PwD ranks are in the tables above.

Admission tips

  • Computer Science closed at JEE Advanced rank 1,439 (All India quota, Round 6).
  • Girls: female-only seats closed at ranks about 1.7× the gender-neutral ranks.
