Indian Institute of Technology Palakkad review: our verdict for 2027

Easier admission Indian Institute of Technology Palakkad is one of the easier IITs to get into in JoSAA. By its open-category closing ranks across branches it ranks #21 of 24 IITs in our 2026 data (bottom 13%). The most competitive seat was Computer Science and Engineering at JEE Advanced rank 7,625; the least competitive was Materials Engineering at 18,397.

Strengths

  • Centrally funded IIT: fees are subsidised and the degree is widely recognised by employers and for higher studies.
  • Placements in figures compiled by College Simplified: avg package ₹14 LPA, median package ₹11 LPA, highest package ₹40 LPA, 79% placed.

Who should apply

Open-category, gender-neutral candidates with a JEE Advanced rank up to about 18,397 had a seat here in 2026; the top branch needed about 7,625 or better. Category ranks, female-only seats and PwD ranks are in the tables above.

Admission tips

  • Computer Science closed at JEE Advanced rank 7,625 (All India quota, Round 6).
  • Branch decides the rank you need: closing ranks ran from 7,625 to 18,397.
  • Girls: female-only seats closed at ranks about 1.5× the gender-neutral ranks.
  • Ranks rose by about 16% from Round 1 to Round 6 for a typical branch, so do not give up after Round 1.
