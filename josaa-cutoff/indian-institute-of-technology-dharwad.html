Indian Institute of Technology Dharwad review: our verdict for 2027

Easier admission Indian Institute of Technology Dharwad is one of the easier IITs to get into in JoSAA. By its open-category closing ranks across branches it ranks #19 of 24 IITs in our 2026 data (bottom 22%). The most competitive seat was Computer Science and Engineering at JEE Advanced rank 8,332; the least competitive was Major in Physics (4 Years, Bachelor of Science) at 18,223.

Strengths

  • Centrally funded IIT: fees are subsidised and the degree is widely recognised by employers and for higher studies.
  • 10 programmes with open seats, so a wide range of ranks can get in.
  • Placements in figures compiled by College Simplified: avg package ₹13 LPA, median package ₹10 LPA, highest package ₹32 LPA, 76% placed.

Who should apply

Open-category, gender-neutral candidates with a JEE Advanced rank up to about 18,223 had a seat here in 2026; the top branch needed about 8,332 or better. Category ranks, female-only seats and PwD ranks are in the tables above.

Admission tips

  • Computer Science closed at JEE Advanced rank 8,332 (All India quota, Round 6).
  • Branch decides the rank you need: closing ranks ran from 8,332 to 18,223.
  • Girls: female-only seats closed at ranks about 1.5× the gender-neutral ranks.
  • Ranks rose by about 15% from Round 1 to Round 6 for a typical branch, so do not give up after Round 1.
