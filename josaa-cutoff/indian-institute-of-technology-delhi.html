Indian Institute of Technology Delhi review: our verdict for 2027

Very high demand Indian Institute of Technology Delhi is one of the most sought-after IITs in JoSAA. By its open-category closing ranks across branches it ranks #1 of 24 IITs in our 2026 data (top 4%). The most competitive seat was Computer Science and Engineering at JEE Advanced rank 128; the least competitive was Design at 12,730.

Strengths

  • Centrally funded IIT: fees are subsidised and the degree is widely recognised by employers and for higher studies.
  • Strong demand: in the top 4% of 24 IITs by closing rank.
  • Computer Science closed at JEE Advanced rank 128 (All India quota, Round 6).
  • 23 programmes with open seats, so a wide range of ranks can get in.
  • Placements in figures compiled by College Simplified: avg package ₹26 LPA, median package ₹22 LPA, highest package ₹2 Cr, 93% placed.

Things to weigh

  • Branch decides the rank you need: closing ranks ran from 128 to 12,730.

Who should apply

Open-category, gender-neutral candidates with a JEE Advanced rank up to about 12,730 had a seat here in 2026; the top branch needed about 128 or better. Category ranks, female-only seats and PwD ranks are in the tables above.

Admission tips

  • Girls: female-only seats closed at ranks about 2.6× the gender-neutral ranks.
