Indian Institute of Technology Mandi review: our verdict for 2027

Moderate demand Indian Institute of Technology Mandi is a mid-range IIT in JoSAA. By its open-category closing ranks across branches it ranks #15 of 24 IITs in our 2026 data (bottom 39%). The most competitive seat was Computer Science and Engineering at JEE Advanced rank 3,762; the least competitive was BS in Chemical Sciences (4 Years, Bachelor of Science) at 18,835.

Strengths

  • Centrally funded IIT: fees are subsidised and the degree is widely recognised by employers and for higher studies.
  • 15 programmes with open seats, so a wide range of ranks can get in.
  • Placements in figures compiled by College Simplified: avg package ₹16 LPA, median package ₹13 LPA, highest package ₹60 LPA, 83% placed.

Who should apply

Open-category, gender-neutral candidates with a JEE Advanced rank up to about 18,835 had a seat here in 2026; the top branch needed about 3,762 or better. Category ranks, female-only seats and PwD ranks are in the tables above.

Admission tips

  • Computer Science closed at JEE Advanced rank 3,762 (All India quota, Round 6).
  • Branch decides the rank you need: closing ranks ran from 3,762 to 18,835.
  • Girls: female-only seats closed at ranks about 1.8× the gender-neutral ranks.
