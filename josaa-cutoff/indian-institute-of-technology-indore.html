Indian Institute of Technology Indore review: our verdict for 2027

Moderate demand Indian Institute of Technology Indore is a mid-range IIT in JoSAA. By its open-category closing ranks across branches it ranks #10 of 24 IITs in our 2026 data (top 42%). The most competitive seat was Computer Science and Engineering at JEE Advanced rank 1,676; the least competitive was Applied and Industrial Chemistry (4 Years, Bachelor of Science) at 15,409.

Strengths

  • Centrally funded IIT: fees are subsidised and the degree is widely recognised by employers and for higher studies.
  • 12 programmes with open seats, so a wide range of ranks can get in.
  • Placements in figures compiled by College Simplified: avg package ₹18 LPA, median package ₹14 LPA, highest package ₹1 Cr, 87% placed.

Things to weigh

  • Branch decides the rank you need: closing ranks ran from 1,676 to 15,409.

Who should apply

Open-category, gender-neutral candidates with a JEE Advanced rank up to about 15,409 had a seat here in 2026; the top branch needed about 1,676 or better. Category ranks, female-only seats and PwD ranks are in the tables above.
