Indian Institute of Technology Bombay review: our verdict for 2027

High demand Indian Institute of Technology Bombay is a highly sought-after IIT in JoSAA. By its open-category closing ranks across branches it ranks #3 of 24 IITs in our 2026 data (top 12%). The most competitive seat was Computer Science and Engineering at JEE Advanced rank 67; the least competitive was Chemistry (4 Years, Bachelor of Science) at 7,838.

Strengths

  • Centrally funded IIT: fees are subsidised and the degree is widely recognised by employers and for higher studies.
  • Strong demand: in the top 12% of 24 IITs by closing rank.
  • Computer Science closed at JEE Advanced rank 67 (All India quota, Round 6).
  • 16 programmes with open seats, so a wide range of ranks can get in.
  • Placements in figures compiled by College Simplified: avg package ₹25 LPA, median package ₹21 LPA, highest package ₹3 Cr, 94% placed.

Things to weigh

  • Branch decides the rank you need: closing ranks ran from 67 to 7,838.

Who should apply

Open-category, gender-neutral candidates with a JEE Advanced rank up to about 7,838 had a seat here in 2026; the top branch needed about 67 or better. Category ranks, female-only seats and PwD ranks are in the tables above.

Admission tips

  • Girls: female-only seats closed at ranks about 2.6× the gender-neutral ranks.
  • Seats fill in Round 1: closing ranks barely moved by Round 6.
