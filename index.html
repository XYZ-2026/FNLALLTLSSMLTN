Frequently Asked Questions — College Simplified 2026

What is College Simplified?
College Simplified is an advanced, data-driven counselling portal designed to help engineering aspirants navigate CAP rounds and institutional admissions. We provide high-precision college predictors, smart preference list builders, category-wise cutoff checkers, and personalized expert mentorship.
Which engineering entrance exams are supported?
We support major national and state-level engineering counselling processes, including MHT-CET (Maharashtra CAP), JoSAA & CSAB (IITs, NITs, IIITs, GFTIs), COMEDK UGET (Karnataka), and Manipal MET.
How accurate are the college predictors?
Our predictors are highly accurate and powered by official round-wise closing cutoff data from previous years. They factor in category reservations, home-state/other-state quotas, shifts, and gender quotas to give you the most precise estimation of your admission chances.
Can I save and export my preference lists?
Yes, standard users can build and save lists up to 10 choices. Premium members can generate unlimited lists, utilize expert-curated templates, reorder via drag-and-drop, and export their finalized list as a professional PDF or Excel sheet.
How do I register for a premium counselling program?
Simply select your desired counselling course/mentorship program from the premium banners on our landing page, enter your email and phone number, complete the payment, and log in using your credentials.
Is my preference list synced across devices?
Yes, all your preference lists, profile details, and checklist items are synced in real-time with Google Firestore, allowing you to access them seamlessly on mobile and desktop.
