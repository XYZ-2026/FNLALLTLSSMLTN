How our data is prepared

College Simplified tools compare your score with past closing cutoffs. They are estimates, not offers of admission.

Pages for 2027 admission use the latest published cutoffs, mostly from 2026. No 2027 cutoffs exist until the 2027 rounds are held, and each table states its own year.

Sources

  • MHT-CET: State CET Cell CAP 2026 cutoff lists for Rounds 1 to 4 and the 2026 seat matrix.
  • All India seats in Maharashtra: CAP 2026 All India cutoff lists. The AIR predictor uses 2025 lists.
  • Direct Second Year: DSE CAP 2026 Rounds 1 and 2.
  • JoSAA and CSAB: 2026 closing ranks.
  • COMEDK UGET: round-wise General Merit and KKR cutoff ranks.
  • Manipal: MET 2025 cutoff ranks.

How the tables are made

MHT-CET tables on college pages use Stage I cutoffs. For each branch we show one open-category seat type: state level (GOPENS) if it exists, otherwise home university (GOPENH), otherwise other than home university (GOPENO). Women’s colleges show the ladies seats (LOPENS, LOPENH, LOPENO). “HU to OHU” and “OHU to HU” mark seats moved between the home-university and other-university pools. Percentiles are rounded to two decimals.

Average packages are figures compiled by College Simplified, not published by the CET Cell. Ask the college for its latest placement report.

Marks vs percentile and percentile vs rank tables are our own estimates. The exam authorities do not publish them.

Limits

Cutoffs change every year with seats, applicants and difficulty. Check the official brochure and portal before you submit an option form: State CET Cell, JoSAA, COMEDK.
